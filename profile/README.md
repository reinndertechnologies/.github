# Reinnder Technologies

Reinnder Technologies is a technology group working toward chips, robots, AI, software and networks, carried from silicon into the field.

The company is at an early stage. Its [twelve areas of work](https://www.reinndertechnologies.com/divisions) are plans, and nothing is on sale yet.

## What we publish

- **[Reinnder Labs](https://www.reinndertechnologies.com/labs):** plain-English guides to fifteen technologies, from AI agents and edge AI to solid-state batteries, 6G and fusion power. Each says what the technology is, how it works, where it stands and what its limits are.
- **Open source:** small parts of our early work, under permissive licences.

| Repository | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [flock-gateway](https://github.com/reinndertechnologies/flock-gateway) | A store-and-forward gateway that keeps sensor readings in order through an internet outage and a restart. MIT licence. | Prototype, tested on a bench only |
